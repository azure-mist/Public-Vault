# Objectives

This course aims to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of the mathematical foundations and applications of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Foundation Models. It covers the mathematical foundations, model architectures, and training methodologies behind key generative models, including Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), diffusion models, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, and MAMBA. The course also explores large-scale Transformer-based architectures such as GPT and BERT. Students will examine the practical applications of these models across various domains, gaining insights into their capabilities, limitations, and broader societal implications. Ethical considerations, responsible deployment, and emerging trends in generative AI development will also be discussed.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在使学生全面理解生成式人工智能（AI）与基础模型的数学基础及其应用。课程涵盖主要生成模型背后的数学基础、模型架构和训练方法，包括生成对抗网络（GAN）、变分自编码器（VAE）、扩散模型、混合专家（MoE）模型和 MAMBA；同时探讨 GPT、BERT 等基于 Transformer 的大规模架构。学生将考察这些模型在不同领域中的实际应用，了解其能力、局限性以及更广泛的社会影响。课程还将讨论伦理考量、负责任部署以及生成式人工智能发展的新趋势。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) explain the mathematical foundations and training principles underlying generative and foundation models, including concepts from probability, optimization, and representation learning;
<font color="#95b3d7">a）解释生成模型和基础模型所依据的数学基础与训练原理，包括概率、优化和表示学习中的相关概念；</font>

b) describe and compare major generative model architectures such as GANs, VAEs, diffusion models, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE), and MAMBA, as well as large-scale Transformer-based models such as GPT and BERT;
<font color="#95b3d7">b）描述并比较 GAN、VAE、扩散模型、混合专家（MoE）和 MAMBA 等主要生成模型架构，以及 GPT、BERT 等基于 Transformer 的大规模模型；</font>

c) analyze the design, training strategies, and application scenarios of generative AI systems across different domains;
<font color="#95b3d7">c）分析不同领域中生成式人工智能系统的设计、训练策略和应用场景；</font>

d) evaluate model performance using concepts such as generalization, capacity, overfitting, underfitting, and regularization;
<font color="#95b3d7">d）运用泛化、容量、过拟合、欠拟合和正则化等概念评估模型性能；</font>

e) assess the limitations, ethical challenges, and societal impacts of deploying generative and foundation models in real-world settings.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）评估在现实环境中部署生成模型和基础模型的局限、伦理挑战与社会影响。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Key topics to be delivered:
<font color="#95b3d7">主要讲授内容包括：</font>

a) Introduction to Generative AI and Foundation Models: Historical development and motivation; fundamental concepts and definitions; key milestones in generative AI research.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）生成式人工智能与基础模型导论：历史发展与研究动机；基本概念和定义；生成式人工智能研究的重要里程碑。</font>

b) Core Generative Architectures: Describe and compare major generative model architectures, including VAEs, GANs, diffusion models, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE), and Mamba, as well as large-scale Transformer-based models such as GPT and BERT.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）核心生成式架构：描述并比较 VAE、GAN、扩散模型、混合专家（MoE）和 Mamba 等主要生成模型架构，以及 GPT、BERT 等基于 Transformer 的大规模模型。</font>

c) Applications and Case Studies: Real-world use cases across domains: text generation (e.g., chatbots, content creation), image synthesis, video generation, and multimodal models. Industrial deployment scenarios and emerging applications will also be examined.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）应用与案例研究：不同领域中的实际用例，包括文本生成（例如聊天机器人和内容创作）、图像合成、视频生成及多模态模型；同时考察工业部署场景和新兴应用。</font>

d) Ethical, Societal, and Future Perspectives: Discussion of key ethical challenges, including fairness, bias, misinformation, and potential misuse. Students will explore regulatory considerations, responsible AI development, and future trends in generative and foundation model research.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）伦理、社会与未来视角：讨论公平、偏见、虚假信息和潜在滥用等主要伦理挑战；探讨监管考量、负责任的人工智能开发，以及生成模型和基础模型研究的未来趋势。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures:
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：</font>

Lectures will introduce foundational theories, model architectures, and key technical results. Mathematical derivations and real-world examples will be used to illustrate concepts.
<font color="#95b3d7">课堂讲授将介绍基础理论、模型架构和关键技术成果，并通过数学推导和现实实例阐释相关概念。</font>

Tutorials:
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课：</font>

Tutorials will focus on hands-on practice, including coding exercises, model training, and group discussions of recent case studies and research papers.
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课将侧重实践，包括编程练习、模型训练，以及围绕近期案例研究和研究论文开展小组讨论。</font>
