# Objectives

1. Raise students’ awareness of the importance of academic integrity and research.
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 提高学生对学术诚信和研究工作重要性的认识。</font>

2. To enable students to develop deeper understanding of Data Science and AI and enhance students’ ability to critically analyse ethical issues and make ethical decisions.
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 使学生加深对数据科学与人工智能的理解，并增强其批判性分析伦理问题和作出伦理决策的能力。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Understand basic concepts and principles of academic integrity and research ethics. Demonstrate awareness and ability to analyse academic integrity and ethical issues.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）理解学术诚信与研究伦理的基本概念和原则，展现对学术诚信及伦理问题的认识和分析能力。</font>

(b) Recognise important ethical issues and practices in a university context. Avoid major misconduct in academic integrity and research ethics, e.g., fabrication, falsification, and plagiarism.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）识别大学环境中的重要伦理问题与实践，避免捏造、篡改和剽窃等严重违反学术诚信及研究伦理的行为。</font>

(c) Understand the implications and concerns on academic integrity raised by the latest technology, and recognise ethical practices in the data processing and result reporting.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）理解最新技术对学术诚信带来的影响与关切，并识别数据处理和结果报告中的合乎伦理的做法。</font>

(d) Analyze and address complex ethical and professional issues within the discipline, and articulate them effectively to stakeholders and the public.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）分析并处理本学科中的复杂伦理与专业问题，并能够向利益相关者和公众作出有效阐述。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

• Introduction to Computer and Mathematical Sciences Ethics - Ethical theories, decision-making frameworks, and the societal impact of data-driven technologies; why integrity matters in data science.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 计算机与数学科学伦理导论 - 伦理理论、决策框架、数据驱动技术对社会的影响，以及诚信为何对数据科学至关重要。</font>

• Academic Integrity and Misconduct - Plagiarism, data fabrication/falsification, code theft; responsible authorship, citation of datasets and code; consequences and prevention.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 学术诚信与不当行为 - 剽窃、数据捏造或篡改、代码盗用；负责任的署名方式、数据集与代码引用；相关后果及预防措施。</font>

• Collaboration and Authorship - Fair credit in team projects; defining contributions in papers, code repositories, and competitions; handling disputes.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 协作与署名 - 团队项目中的公平署名；明确论文、代码仓库和竞赛中的贡献；处理争议。</font>

• Privacy, Consent, and Human Subjects - Informed consent, anonymization, de-identification; GDPR and IRB essentials for data involving people.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 隐私、同意与人类受试者 - 知情同意、匿名化与去标识化；涉及个人数据时的 GDPR 和 IRB 基本要求。</font>

• Bias, Fairness, and Discrimination - Sources of bias in data and models; fairness metrics; auditing and mitigating harmful outcomes; real-world case studies.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 偏见、公平与歧视 - 数据和模型中的偏见来源；公平性指标；审查并缓解有害结果；现实案例研究。</font>

• Intellectual Property and Open Science - Licensing datasets and code (MIT, GPL, CC); copyrights vs. patents in algorithms; balancing openness and protection.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 知识产权与开放科学 - 数据集和代码的许可证（MIT、GPL、CC）；算法中的版权与专利；在开放和保护之间取得平衡。</font>

• Artificial Intelligence (AI) and GenAI Research Discuss current pitfalls of AI research and usages of AI platforms for research.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 人工智能（AI）与生成式人工智能研究 - 讨论当前人工智能研究中的问题，以及在研究中使用人工智能平台的方式。</font>

• Ethical Use of AI and Generative Tools - Hallucinations, bias amplification, plagiarism risks; transparency, accountability, and regulations; responsible use in assignments and research.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 合乎伦理地使用人工智能和生成式工具 - 幻觉、偏见放大和剽窃风险；透明度、问责与法规；在作业和研究中的负责任使用。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures: Formal classroom lectures will be given to introduce the concepts of research ethics in Computer and Mathematical Sciences. Principles and examples will be explained to highlight the ethical issues. Students need to achieve 100% attendance in the lectures to pass the subject.
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：课程将通过正式课堂讲授介绍计算机与数学科学中的研究伦理概念，并通过原则说明和实例分析突出相关伦理问题。学生必须达到 100% 的课堂出席率方可通过本课程。</font>
