# Objectives

The objective of this course is to equip students with the mathematical foundations and practical skills of Reinforcement Learning (RL), a core subfield of artificial intelligence. The curriculum will cover mathematical foundations of RL, key RL algorithms, computational tools, and real-world applications, emphasizing both the theoretical foundations and problem-solving skills in dynamic environments. Students will learn to analyze the benefits, challenges, and ethical considerations of RL deployment in various industries.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在使学生掌握强化学习（RL）这一人工智能核心子领域的数学基础与实践技能。课程将涵盖强化学习的数学基础、关键算法、计算工具和现实世界应用，并同时强调理论基础以及在动态环境中解决问题的能力。学生将学习分析强化学习在不同行业部署时的优势、挑战与伦理考量。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Know the foundation concepts of reinforcement learning and the relevant algorithms
<font color="#95b3d7">a）了解强化学习的基础概念及相关算法。</font>

b) Understand various RL algorithms (e.g., Q-learning, Policy Gradients, DQN) and their strengths and weaknesses.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）理解各种强化学习算法（例如 Q-learning、策略梯度和 DQN）及其优缺点。</font>

c) Apply the reinforcement learning algorithms for practical problems using programming frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow).
<font color="#95b3d7">c）使用编程框架（例如 PyTorch、TensorFlow）将强化学习算法应用于实际问题。</font>

d) Derive insights from RL real-world applications in domains such as robotics, gaming, healthcare, and autonomous systems.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）从强化学习在机器人、游戏、医疗和自主系统等领域的现实应用中提炼见解。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Key topics to be delivered:
<font color="#95b3d7">主要讲授内容包括：</font>

a) The development history of RL and its integration with deep learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）强化学习的发展历史及其与深度学习的融合。</font>

b) Mathematical foundations and core RL concepts: state spaces, action spaces, reward functions, Markov Decision Processes (MDPs).
<font color="#95b3d7">b）数学基础与强化学习核心概念：状态空间、动作空间、奖励函数和马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）。</font>

c) Key RL algorithms and their mathematical guarantees: Value-based methods (Q-learning, SARSA), Policy-based methods (REINFORCE, Actor-Critic), Model-based RL.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）关键强化学习算法及其数学保证：基于价值的方法（Q-learning、SARSA）、基于策略的方法（REINFORCE、Actor-Critic）和基于模型的强化学习。</font>

d) Advanced RL techniques: exploration-exploitation trade-offs, experience replay, multi-agent RL, hierarchical RL, RLHF
<font color="#95b3d7">d）高级强化学习技术：探索与利用的权衡、经验回放、多智能体强化学习、分层强化学习和 RLHF。</font>

e) Case studies: RL in game AI (e.g., AlphaGo), robotic control, recommendation systems, and resource optimization.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）案例研究：强化学习在游戏人工智能（例如 AlphaGo）、机器人控制、推荐系统和资源优化中的应用。</font>

f) Selected advanced topics: sample efficiency, convergence stability, reward engineering, federated RL, and ethical considerations.
<font color="#95b3d7">f）高级专题选讲：样本效率、收敛稳定性、奖励工程、联邦强化学习和伦理考量。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures:
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：</font>

Introduce RL theory, algorithm derivations, and industry applications, with a focus on mathematical foundations and technical frameworks.
<font color="#95b3d7">介绍强化学习理论、算法推导和行业应用，重点讲解数学基础与技术框架。</font>

Tutorials:
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课：</font>

Hands-on coding sessions and group discussions on case studies, using real-world datasets and simulation environments (e.g., OpenAI Gym, MuJoCo).
<font color="#95b3d7">使用现实世界的数据集和模拟环境（例如 OpenAI Gym、MuJoCo），开展动手编程练习以及案例研究小组讨论。</font>
