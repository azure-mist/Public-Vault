# Objectives

To introduce students some fundamental building blocks in blockchain technology as well as its application in cryptocurrencies, stablecoins, and decentralized finance. Help students to develop practical programming, statistics and mathematical skills that are valued in the FinTech industry.
<font color="#95b3d7">向学生介绍区块链技术的若干基本构件，以及区块链在加密货币、稳定币和去中心化金融中的应用；帮助学生培养金融科技行业重视的编程、统计和数学实践技能。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Acquire knowledge of the core ideas in FinTech as well as develop hands-on programming skills in the Fintech area.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）掌握金融科技的核心理念，并培养金融科技领域的编程实践技能。</font>

(b) Analyze financial problems, and identify and define the computing technologies appropriate to its solution.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）分析金融问题，并识别和确定适合解决这些问题的计算技术。</font>

(c) Gain experience in designing and implementing smart contracts and decentralized applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）积累设计和实现智能合约与去中心化应用的经验。</font>

(d) Understand the blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）理解区块链技术与加密货币。</font>

(e) Reflect the quantitative skills to solve other financial applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">（e）展现运用定量技能解决其他金融应用问题的能力。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will mainly be delivered through lectures and exercise-based tutorials. The teaching and learning approach is mainly problem-solving oriented. The approach aims at the development of financial modeling and quantitative skills and how the techniques can be applied to applications in financial technology. Students are encouraged to adopt a deep study approach by employing high level cognitive strategies, such as critical and evaluative thinking, relating, integrating and applying theories to practice.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和以练习为基础的导修课进行。教学与学习以解决问题为导向，旨在培养金融建模和定量分析技能，并说明如何将这些技术应用于金融科技。课程鼓励学生运用批判性思考、评价性思考、联系、整合以及将理论应用于实践等高阶认知策略，采取深度学习方式。</font>
