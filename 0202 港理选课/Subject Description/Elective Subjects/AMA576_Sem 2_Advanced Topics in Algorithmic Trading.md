# Objectives

To introduce students some basic concepts and methods in stochastic modeling of market microstructure and high frequency trading. To provide a comprehensive view of some key mathematical foundations of algorithmic trading strategies including stochastic control and optimal execution. To enable students to master some implementation methods of these practical algorithmic trading using financial data and software.
<font color="#95b3d7">向学生介绍市场微观结构与高频交易随机建模中的若干基本概念和方法；全面介绍算法交易策略的一些关键数学基础，包括随机控制和最优执行；使学生掌握利用金融数据和软件实现这些实用算法交易方法的若干途径。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Develop quantitative skills to interpret and analyze market microstructure and trading behavior;
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）培养解释和分析市场微观结构与交易行为的定量技能；</font>

(b) Develop theoretical knowledge to identify, define and formulate some mathematical problems related to high frequency trading;
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）掌握相关理论知识，以识别、定义和表述若干高频交易相关数学问题；</font>

(c) Master stochastic control methods to solve some optimal liquidation and optimal execution problems related to high frequency trading;
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）掌握随机控制方法，以求解若干与高频交易相关的最优清算和最优执行问题；</font>

(d) Produce basic numerical implementations of some practical algorithmic trading strategies using the historical financial data and software.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）使用历史金融数据和软件，对若干实用算法交易策略进行基本的数值实现。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Market Microstructure and Trading Mechanism:
<font color="#95b3d7">市场微观结构与交易机制：</font>

Electronic market, market participants, trading types, trading costs, limit order books, measuring liquidity, asset prices and returns intraday, inter-arrival times, latency and tick size, market fragmentation, daily volume and volatility and intraday activity, trading signals, trading cycles, backtesting, performance criteria, statistical data analysis
<font color="#95b3d7">电子市场、市场参与者、交易类型、交易成本、限价订单簿、流动性度量、日内资产价格与收益、到达间隔时间、延迟与最小报价单位、市场碎片化、每日交易量与波动率和日内活动、交易信号、交易周期、回测、绩效标准、统计数据分析。</font>

Algorithmic Trading:
<font color="#95b3d7">算法交易：</font>

Liquidation without penalties, liquidation with temporary and permanent price impact, liquidation with only limit orders, liquidation with limit and market orders, introduction to numerical methods in algorithmic trading using the Bloomberg database, reinforcement learning algorithms, deep learning algorithms
<font color="#95b3d7">无惩罚清算、考虑暂时性和永久性价格冲击的清算、仅使用限价单的清算、结合限价单与市价单的清算、使用 Bloomberg 数据库的算法交易数值方法导论、强化学习算法、深度学习算法。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will mainly be delivered through lectures and lab-based tutorials. The lectures will be conducted to introduce the theoretical background of algorithmic trading, mathematical foundations of stochastic models. The compute lab tutorials will provide the practical use of software and Bloomberg terminal.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和实验室导修课进行。课堂讲授将介绍算法交易的理论背景和随机模型的数学基础；计算机实验室导修课将提供软件和 Bloomberg 终端的实践使用机会。</font>

Explanation of the appropriateness of the assessment methods in assessing the intended learning outcomes:
<font color="#95b3d7">关于这些评估方法在衡量预期学习成果方面的适用性说明：</font>

This subject focuses on both the mathematical foundation and practical implementation of high frequency trading. Some of the algorithms are based on important mathematical models such as stochastic control and related optimal execution methods. Thus, Exam-based assessment is the most appropriate assessment method, including 20% midterm test and 50% examination.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程同时关注高频交易的数学基础与实践实现。部分算法以随机控制和相关最优执行方法等重要数学模型为基础。因此，以考试为基础的评估最为合适，包括占 20% 的期中测试和占 50% 的考试。</font>

As this subject also emphasizes the practical side of market making and trading strategies, a mini-project that takes the weight of 20% is appropriate for assessing the intended learning outcomes (d). Moreover, 10% worth of assignments are included as a component of continuous assessment so as to keep students in progress.
<font color="#95b3d7">由于本课程也强调做市与交易策略的实践层面，占 20% 的小型项目适合用于评估预期学习成果（d）。此外，持续评估还包括占 10% 的作业，以持续检验学生的学习进度。</font>

Continuous Assessment comprises of assignments, mini-project and test. A written examination is held at the end of the semester.
<font color="#95b3d7">持续评估包括作业、小型项目和测试；学期结束时还会举行一次笔试。</font>
