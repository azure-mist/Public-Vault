# Objectives

The subject is aims to provide a multidisciplinary study on neuroscience and computing, enabling students to understand the fundamentals of brain function and apply them to design innovative computing systems. It will primarily focus on exploring the computational principles, models, hardware systems, and applications derived from the human and animal brains.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在开展神经科学与计算的多学科学习，使学生理解脑功能基础，并将其应用于创新计算系统的设计。课程将主要探索源自人脑和动物脑的计算原理、模型、硬件系统与应用。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a. Understand the operating principles and models of biological neurons
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 理解生物神经元的运行原理与模型。</font>

b. Understand the learning algorithms underpinning neural computation
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 理解支撑神经计算的学习算法。</font>

c. Understand the principles and models for visual systems, auditory system, motor system, and memory system, etc.
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 理解视觉系统、听觉系统、运动系统和记忆系统等的原理与模型。</font>

d. Familiar with the neuromorphic computing hardware and systems
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 熟悉神经形态计算硬件与系统。</font>

e. Familiar with the techniques for brain-signal analysis and able to apply them to analyze and interpret brain signals
<font color="#95b3d7">e. 熟悉脑信号分析技术，并能够运用这些技术分析和解释脑信号。</font>

f. Able to design brain-inspired model and systems to solve real-world pattern recognition problems
<font color="#95b3d7">f. 能够设计类脑模型和系统，以解决现实世界中的模式识别问题。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

1. Lectures and Seminars
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 课堂讲授与研讨会</font>

The concepts and introductions on brain-inspired computing will be given via lectures. There will be in-class activities (e.g., discussions and exercises) to better engage students in active learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">类脑计算的概念和导论将通过课堂讲授介绍；课程还将安排讨论和练习等课堂活动，以促进学生主动学习。</font>

2. Labs and Tutorials
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 实验课与导修课</font>

Hands-on experience will be given via labs and tutorials to reinforce the concepts learned and gain practical skills in brain-signal analysis and brain-inspired computing.
<font color="#95b3d7">实验课和导修课将提供动手实践，以巩固所学概念，并培养脑信号分析与类脑计算方面的实践技能。</font>

39 hours of class activities including - lectures, tutorials, lab, and seminars where applicable.
<font color="#95b3d7">共安排 39 小时的课堂活动，包括适用情况下的讲授、导修课、实验课和研讨会。</font>
