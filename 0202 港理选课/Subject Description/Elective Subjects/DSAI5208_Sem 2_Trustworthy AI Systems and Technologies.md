# Objectives

• Provide students with knowledge of the foundations of trustworthy AI systems and technologies.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生掌握可信人工智能系统与技术的基础知识。</font>

• Provide students with advanced knowledge of a wide spectrum of techniques to establish trust and security for AI-driven systems.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生掌握为人工智能驱动系统建立可信性与安全性的广泛高级技术知识。</font>

• Train students to apply advanced AI techniques to ensure trust and safeguard security in contemporary technological environments.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 训练学生应用高级人工智能技术，在当代技术环境中确保可信性并保障安全。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Have a good understanding of the trust, security, and privacy-related challenges in the AI-enabled systems.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）充分理解人工智能赋能系统中与可信性、安全和隐私相关的挑战。</font>

b) Have a deep understanding of the techniques and requirements for the AI-enabled biometric systems.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）深入理解人工智能赋能生物识别系统的技术和要求。</font>

c) Critically review and consolidate the skills for establishing trustworthy AI systems and security; and
<font color="#95b3d7">c）批判性地审视并巩固构建可信人工智能系统和保障安全所需的技能；以及</font>

d) Conduct a real-world case study and performance analysis in processing biometric data using advanced AI techniques.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）运用高级人工智能技术处理生物识别数据，并开展现实案例研究与性能分析。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

1. Foundations of Trustworthy AI:
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 可信人工智能基础：</font>

Principles of Trustworthy AI, Security and Fairness in AI Systems, Threats to Trustworthy AI like Backdoor Attacks, Adversarial Attacks and Mitigation, Deepfakes, Hyper Realistic Deepfakes with Diffusion Models, Face-Swapping, Audio Deepfakes, AI Ethics, Trust Erosion and Manipulation.
<font color="#95b3d7">可信人工智能原理、人工智能系统中的安全与公平、后门攻击等可信人工智能威胁、对抗攻击与缓解、深度伪造、使用扩散模型生成的超逼真深度伪造、换脸、音频深度伪造、人工智能伦理、信任侵蚀与操纵。</font>

2. Defence Mechanisms for AI-generated Content:
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 人工智能生成内容的防御机制：</font>

CNN and LLM-based Detectors, Watermarking and Cryptographic Solutions, Blockchain for provenance (e.g., video authenticator), and Fake audio detection via spectrogram analysis and linguistic patterns. Legal and Policy Frameworks.
<font color="#95b3d7">基于 CNN 和 LLM 的检测器、水印与密码学解决方案、用于来源追踪的区块链（例如视频认证器），以及通过频谱图分析和语言模式检测伪造音频；法律与政策框架。</font>

3. Privacy-Preserving AI
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 隐私保护人工智能</font>

Federated Learning, Differential Privacy and Homomorphic Encryption, Global regulations like the EU’s AI Act, Challenges in Enforcement and Jurisdiction.
<font color="#95b3d7">联邦学习、差分隐私与同态加密、欧盟《人工智能法案》等全球法规，以及执法与司法管辖方面的挑战。</font>

4. Trustworthy Security using Biometrics:
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 使用生物识别实现可信安全：</font>

Introduction to Biometrics, Desirable Properties of Biometrics for Practical Systems, Strengths and Weaknesses of Different Biometric Patterns, Behavioral Biometrics, Privacy Concerns and Social Challenges.
<font color="#95b3d7">生物识别导论、实用系统中生物识别应具备的理想特性、不同生物识别模式的优缺点、行为生物识别、隐私关切与社会挑战。</font>

5. Performance Evaluation and Applications:
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 性能评估与应用：</font>

Error Rates and Performance Plots for Trustworthy Security Systems, Performance Evaluation for Real World Systems and Applications, Presentation Attacks and Detection.
<font color="#95b3d7">可信安全系统的错误率和性能图、现实系统与应用的性能评估、呈现攻击及其检测。</font>

6. Multimodal Systems:
<font color="#95b3d7">6. 多模态系统：</font>

Advantage of Multimodal Systems over Unimodal Systems for Trustworthy AI, Multimodal and Score Normalization Techniques, Feature, Score, and Rank Level Fusion.
<font color="#95b3d7">在可信人工智能中多模态系统相对于单模态系统的优势、多模态与分数归一化技术、特征级、分数级和排序级融合。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The course material will be delivered as a combination of lectures, tutorials, and small group projects. Students will get familiar with basic concepts and technologies of trustworthy AI systems, biometric data processing, security, and applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">课程材料将通过课堂讲授、导修课和小组项目相结合的方式讲授。学生将熟悉可信人工智能系统、生物识别数据处理、安全与应用的基本概念和技术。</font>
