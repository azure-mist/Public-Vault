# Objectives

The objectives of this subject are to:
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程的目标是：</font>

1. introduce students the concept and challenge of big data;
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 向学生介绍大数据的概念与挑战；</font>

2. teach students in applying skills and tools to manage and analyze the big data.
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 教授学生运用相关技能和工具管理与分析大数据。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a. understand the concept and challenge of big data and why traditional technology is inadequate to analyze the big data;
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 理解大数据的概念与挑战，以及传统技术为何不足以分析大数据；</font>

b. understand how to collect, manage, store, and query various form of big data;
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 理解如何收集、管理、存储和查询各种形式的大数据；</font>

c. familiar with the classical data analysis and machine learning algorithms;
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 熟悉经典数据分析与机器学习算法；</font>

d. familiar with large-scale analytics tools to solve some open big data problems; and
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 熟悉大规模分析工具，以解决若干开放式大数据问题；以及</font>

e. analyze the impact of big data for real-world business decisions and strategy.
<font color="#95b3d7">e. 分析大数据对现实商业决策与战略的影响。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

1. Introduction to Big Data: Different V’s, their challenges and application domains.
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 大数据导论：不同的 V 特征、相关挑战与应用领域。</font>

2. Cloud Computing Basics: Software as a service (SaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Desktop as a Service (DaaS), Public, Private and Enterprise Cloud.
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 云计算基础：软件即服务（SaaS）、平台即服务（PaaS）、基础设施即服务（IaaS）、桌面即服务（DaaS），以及公有云、私有云和企业云。</font>

3. Big Data Computing: Concepts, Platform, Service, and Tools
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 大数据计算：概念、平台、服务与工具</font>

4. Large-Scale Programming Abstraction: MapReduce and its open source implementation of Hadoop
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 大规模编程抽象：MapReduce 及其开源实现 Hadoop</font>

5. Large-Scale Data Processing Framework: Apache Spark and its Built-in Modules
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 大规模数据处理框架：Apache Spark 及其内置模块</font>

6. Large-Scale Database Management: NoSQL and other tools, e.g. MongoDB, Google BigTable, etc.
<font color="#95b3d7">6. 大规模数据库管理：NoSQL 及 MongoDB、Google BigTable 等其他工具。</font>

7. Machine Learning Systems for Big Data: Methods and Tools
<font color="#95b3d7">7. 面向大数据的机器学习系统：方法与工具</font>

8. Big Data Visualization: Data types and dimensions; Visual encoding and perception
<font color="#95b3d7">8. 大数据可视化：数据类型与维度；视觉编码与感知</font>

9. Big Data Case Studies
<font color="#95b3d7">9. 大数据案例研究</font>

<font color="#95b3d7">综合采用课堂讲授、讨论和案例研究。</font>

Class activities include lectures and tutorials.
<font color="#95b3d7">课堂活动包括讲授和导修课。</font>
