# Objectives

The objective of this course is to introduce students to the fundamental principles of learning theory that underpin modern artificial intelligence (AI) systems. The course covers core mathematical concepts, theoretical foundations, and recent advances, enabling students to understand, analyze, and evaluate learning algorithms from a rigorous perspective. Practical implications for the design and assessment of AI models will also be discussed.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在向学生介绍支撑现代人工智能（AI）系统的学习理论基本原理。课程涵盖核心数学概念、理论基础和近期进展，使学生能够从严谨视角理解、分析和评估学习算法；同时还将讨论这些理论对人工智能模型设计与评估的实际意义。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) explain key mathematical foundations of statistical learning theory and their relevance to AI;
<font color="#95b3d7">a）解释统计学习理论的主要数学基础及其与人工智能的关系；</font>

b) describe and analyze popular learning paradigms, such as supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning;
<font color="#95b3d7">b）描述并分析监督学习、无监督学习和强化学习等常见学习范式；</font>

c) evaluate the generalization ability of learning algorithms and understand the trade-offs involved;
<font color="#95b3d7">c）评估学习算法的泛化能力，并理解其中涉及的权衡；</font>

d) apply basic concepts of capacity, overfitting, underfitting, and regularization in AI models;
<font color="#95b3d7">d）在人工智能模型中应用容量、过拟合、欠拟合和正则化等基本概念；</font>

e) critically assess theoretical guarantees and limitations of machine learning methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）批判性地评估机器学习方法的理论保证与局限。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Key topics to be delivered:
<font color="#95b3d7">主要讲授内容包括：</font>

a) Learning Theory Foundations for AI: Motivation, history, and core concepts that underpin modern AI applications, from vision to language models.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）人工智能学习理论基础：支撑从视觉到语言模型等现代人工智能应用的研究动机、历史与核心概念。</font>

b) Supervised Learning and Generalization: Key ideas like empirical risk minimization, bias-variance trade-off, and generalization bounds, with relevance to training robust models.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）监督学习与泛化：经验风险最小化、偏差-方差权衡和泛化界等关键思想，以及它们与训练稳健模型的关系。</font>

c) Model Complexity and Regularization: VC-dimension, Rademacher complexity, and regularization techniques, applied to control overfitting in real-world systems.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）模型复杂度与正则化：VC 维、Rademacher 复杂度和正则化技术，并将其用于控制现实系统中的过拟合。</font>

d) Beyond Supervised Learning: Theoretical insights into unsupervised and reinforcement learning, linked to applications like clustering and decision-making.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）超越监督学习：无监督学习和强化学习的理论见解，并联系聚类和决策等应用。</font>

e) Advanced Topics and Limitations: Topics include PAC learning, uniform convergence, algorithmic stability, and theoretical gaps that highlight the current limitations of AI models. The module will also explore emerging research directions in theoretical deep learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）高级专题与局限：包括 PAC 学习、一致收敛、算法稳定性，以及揭示当前人工智能模型局限的理论缺口。本单元还将探讨理论深度学习中的新兴研究方向。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures:
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：</font>

Lectures will introduce fundamental theoretical concepts, key results, and their practical implications for AI. Mathematical proofs will be presented where appropriate, with emphasis on intuition and applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">课堂讲授将介绍基础理论概念、关键结果及其对人工智能的实际意义；在适当之处给出数学证明，并强调直观理解与应用。</font>

Tutorials:
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课：</font>

Tutorials will reinforce theoretical understanding through problem sets, in-class exercises, and discussions. Selected readings and case analyses will be used to bridge theory and real-world AI applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课将通过习题、课堂练习和讨论巩固理论理解，并通过精选阅读和案例分析连接理论与现实人工智能应用。</font>
