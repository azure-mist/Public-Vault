# Objectives

• To let the students learn the fundamental principles on the aspects of interdisciplinary research including acquiring, processing, analyzing, understanding and utilizing high-dimensional visual data from the real world;
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生学习跨学科研究各环节的基本原理，包括获取、处理、分析、理解和利用现实世界中的高维视觉数据；</font>

• To equip the students with the knowledge of how to develop artificial intelligent systems which automate tasks that the human visual system can do;
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生掌握开发人工智能系统的知识，从而自动执行人类视觉系统能够完成的任务；</font>

• To guide the students to understand the relevant state of art technologies and gain experience throughout a variety of case studies.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 引导学生理解相关的最先进技术，并通过多种案例研究积累经验。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a. critically review and consolidate various methods for digital image processing and analysis and relate or apply them to different applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 批判性地审视并整合各种数字图像处理与分析方法，并将其联系或应用于不同场景。</font>

b. grasp various algorithms for vision related tasks and develop creative solutions to practical problems by considering various requirements and complex issues (e.g., legal/social/ethics issues).
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 掌握面向视觉相关任务的各种算法，并在考虑不同需求和复杂问题（例如法律、社会和伦理问题）的基础上，为实际问题提出创新解决方案。</font>

c. carry out in-depth analysis of the digital image data with different image data models, pattern recognition algorithms and learning theory.
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 运用不同的图像数据模型、模式识别算法和学习理论，对数字图像数据开展深入分析。</font>

d. demonstrate leadership and qualities of reflective practitioners by engaging in a group project on vision related topics and various learning activities.
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 通过参与视觉相关主题的小组项目及各种学习活动，展现领导能力和反思型实践者的素质。</font>

e. communicate effectively to peers and senior colleagues and present quality deliverables through reading and writing reports and documents.
<font color="#95b3d7">e. 通过阅读及撰写报告和文档，与同伴和资深同事进行有效沟通，并提交高质量成果。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

1. Introduction
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 导论</font>

a. The background
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 背景</font>

b. An overview of the related fields.
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 相关领域概述。</font>

c. The development of vision systems
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 视觉系统的发展</font>

2. Image acquisition and formation models
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 图像采集与成像模型</font>

a. Imaging systems
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 成像系统</font>

b. Geometry of image formation
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 图像形成的几何原理</font>

c. Camera models and calibration
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 相机模型与标定</font>

d. Orthographic and perspective projections
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 正交投影与透视投影</font>

3. The fundamentals of image processing
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 图像处理基础</font>

a. Image enhancement
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 图像增强</font>

b. Image representation
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 图像表示</font>

c. Image transformations
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 图像变换</font>

4. The fundamentals of deep learning
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 深度学习基础</font>

a. Basic concept of machine learning
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 机器学习的基本概念</font>

b. Deep neural network
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 深度神经网络</font>

c. Transformer
<font color="#95b3d7">c. Transformer</font>

5. 2D Vision
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 二维视觉</font>

a. Image feature, matching and retrieval
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 图像特征、匹配与检索</font>

b. Image segmentation, understanding and object recognition
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 图像分割、理解与目标识别</font>

c. Optical flow, video tracking and motion detection
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 光流、视频跟踪与运动检测</font>

d. Image and video generation
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 图像与视频生成</font>

6. 3D vision
<font color="#95b3d7">6. 三维视觉</font>

a. The geometry of multiple views
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 多视图几何</font>

b. Reconstruction: Shape from multiple views and single view.
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 重建：从多视图和单视图恢复形状。</font>

c. 3D Scene understanding
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 三维场景理解</font>

7. Applications: Industry, mapping, robots and games.
<font color="#95b3d7">7. 应用：工业、测绘、机器人和游戏。</font>

8. Professionalism and legal/social/ethics issues related to computer vision (e.g., personal data (privacy) ordinance)
<font color="#95b3d7">8. 与计算机视觉相关的专业素养及法律、社会和伦理问题（例如个人资料（隐私）条例）。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures provide students with the content of main concepts and methods of the course, together with comprehensive examples, and class questions/answers/discussions for easy understanding.
<font color="#95b3d7">课堂讲授向学生介绍课程的主要概念与方法，并结合综合实例以及课堂问答和讨论帮助学生理解。</font>

Tutorials and lab sessions offer the opportunity for students to review and consolidate the lecture and reference materials through exercises and software tools.
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课和实验课使学生有机会通过练习与软件工具复习并巩固课堂和参考材料。</font>

Project assignments will give students the opportunity to solve practical visual information analysis problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">项目作业将使学生有机会解决实际的视觉信息分析问题。</font>

Written assignments help students to develop a solid foundation of visual information processing and analysis throughout the project work of case studies.
<font color="#95b3d7">书面作业帮助学生在案例研究项目中建立扎实的视觉信息处理与分析基础。</font>
