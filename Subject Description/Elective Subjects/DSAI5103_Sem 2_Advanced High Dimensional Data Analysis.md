# Objectives

To provide a comprehensive introduction to high dimensional data analysis. To present fundamental concepts and algorithms for selected topics in data mining, to provide the students with the necessary background for the applications to real problems, and to provide a starting point for students who are interested in pursuing research in data science or related fields.
<font color="#95b3d7">全面介绍高维数据分析；讲授数据挖掘选定专题的基本概念和算法，为学生将其应用于现实问题提供必要背景，并为有意从事数据科学或相关领域研究的学生提供起点。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Demonstrate mastery of the principles of high dimensional data analysis.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）展现对高维数据分析原理的熟练掌握。</font>

(b) Develop quantitative skills of data analysis and interpret the outcomes of algorithms.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）培养数据分析方面的定量技能，并解释算法的结果。</font>

(c) Identify, define, and formulate problems of high dimensional data analysis in real applications and generate workable solutions to problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）识别、定义并表述现实应用中的高维数据分析问题，并针对这些问题提出可行的解决方案。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Multivariate normal distribution, Estimation of the mean vector and covariance matrix, Multiple and partial correlation coefficients; Estimation of high dimensional sparse parameters (Regularization, threshold estimators); Principal component analysis; Classification and Clustering; Regularized linear regression; Least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO); Choice of tuning parameters; Selected topics (e.g. FDR, Screening, Knockoffs).
<font color="#95b3d7">多元正态分布、均值向量与协方差矩阵估计、多重相关系数与偏相关系数；高维稀疏参数估计（正则化、阈值估计量）；主成分分析；分类与聚类；正则化线性回归；最小绝对收缩与选择算子（LASSO）；调节参数的选择；选定专题（例如 FDR、筛选、Knockoffs）。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will mainly be delivered through lectures and tutorials in computer lab. The theoretical background and the real applications of learning algorithms are both emphasized.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和计算机实验室导修课进行，同时强调学习算法的理论背景和现实应用。</font>
