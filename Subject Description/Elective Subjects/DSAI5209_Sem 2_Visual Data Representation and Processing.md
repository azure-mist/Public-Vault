# Objectives

• Provide students with knowledge of the fundamentals of visual data representation, encoding, and compression techniques.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生掌握视觉数据表示、编码和压缩技术的基础知识。</font>

• Provide students with knowledge of a wide spectrum of visual data protection and security techniques.
<font color="#95b3d7">• 使学生掌握广泛的视觉数据保护与安全技术。</font>

• Train students to apply advanced AI techniques to visual data processing
<font color="#95b3d7">• 训练学生将高级人工智能技术应用于视觉数据处理。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Have a good understanding of visual data representation, encoding, and compression techniques.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）充分理解视觉数据表示、编码和压缩技术。</font>

b) Have a deep understanding of the techniques and requirements for the protection of visual data.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）深入理解视觉数据保护的技术和要求。</font>

c) Critically review and consolidate the skills for problem solving involving visual data; and
<font color="#95b3d7">c）批判性地审视并巩固解决视觉数据相关问题的技能；以及</font>

d) Conduct a real-world case study in processing visual data using advanced AI techniques.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）运用高级人工智能技术处理视觉数据，并开展现实案例研究。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

1. Image and Video Representation
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 图像与视频表示</font>

Common visual data augmentation methods and representation formats, Sampling and Quantization, Visual Data Interpolation and Frame Resolution Enhancement Methods, Distortion Measurement, Rate-Distortion Theory
<font color="#95b3d7">常用视觉数据增强方法与表示格式、采样与量化、视觉数据插值和帧分辨率增强方法、失真度量、率失真理论。</font>

2. Color and Audio Representation
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 色彩与音频表示</font>

Color and Audio Fundamentals, CMY and CMYK Color Models, RGB and HIS Representations, Color Models and Processing
<font color="#95b3d7">色彩与音频基础、CMY 和 CMYK 颜色模型、RGB 和 HIS 表示、颜色模型与处理。</font>

3. Video Compression Fundamentals
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 视频压缩基础</font>

Lossless Predictive Coding, Lossy Visual Data Compression using Discrete Cosine Transform and Wavelet based Encoding, Color Models in Videos and Representation, Compression Techniques, Standards like JPEG, MPEG, etc.
<font color="#95b3d7">无损预测编码、使用离散余弦变换和基于小波编码的有损视觉数据压缩、视频中的颜色模型与表示、压缩技术、JPEG 和 MPEG 等标准。</font>

4. Video Processing
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 视频处理</font>

Block Based Motion Estimation, 2D Logarithmic search, Hierarchical search, video encoding standards, Forward and Bidirectional Prediction, Group of Frames, Transmission and Display Sequencing in Videos
<font color="#95b3d7">基于块的运动估计、二维对数搜索、分层搜索、视频编码标准、前向与双向预测、帧组、视频中的传输和显示顺序。</font>

5. Object Detection in Videos
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 视频中的目标检测</font>

Bounding boxes, Performance metrics for Object detection like Intersection over Union, Precision, Recall, etc. Common object detection models like YOLO or SOLO, and foundation models like SAM or SE, Semantic Segmentation
<font color="#95b3d7">边界框、目标检测性能指标（例如交并比、精确率、召回率等）、YOLO 或 SOLO 等常见目标检测模型、SAM 或 SE 等基础模型、语义分割。</font>

6. Image and Video Content Protection
<font color="#95b3d7">6. 图像与视频内容保护</font>

Video Watermarking for Rights Protection and Monitoring, Spatial and Spectral Domain Techniques, Content Identification and Security.
<font color="#95b3d7">用于权利保护与监测的视频水印、空间域与频域技术、内容识别与安全。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The course material will be delivered as a combination of lectures, tutorials, and small group projects. Students will get familiar with basic concepts and technologies of visual data processing, representations and applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">课程材料将通过课堂讲授、导修课和小组项目相结合的方式讲授。学生将熟悉视觉数据处理、表示和应用的基本概念与技术。</font>
