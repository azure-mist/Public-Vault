# Objectives

This subject introduces students fundamental theories and methods of data mining and stochastic models that are useful for analyzing environmental data and carbon trading.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程向学生介绍数据挖掘和随机模型的基础理论与方法，这些内容可用于分析环境数据和碳交易。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) understand the principle of environmental policy and carbon market;
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）理解环境政策与碳市场的原理；</font>

(b) understand the principle of data analytics and data mining, apply various data analytics algorithms and techniques to analyze data related to carbon neutrality;
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）理解数据分析和数据挖掘的原理，并运用各种数据分析算法与技术分析碳中和相关数据；</font>

(c) understand different stochastic models for carbon trading
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）理解用于碳交易的不同随机模型。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Introduction to the Theory of Environmental Policy: Basic concepts in environmental economics in understanding the principle of environmental policies.
<font color="#95b3d7">环境政策理论导论：用于理解环境政策原理的环境经济学基本概念。</font>

Overview of statistics and applied probability: Basic concepts in statistics and probability, point estimation, hypothesis testing.
<font color="#95b3d7">统计学与应用概率概述：统计与概率的基本概念、点估计、假设检验。</font>

Statistical data mining for environmental data: Data analysis methods include principal component analysis, linear regression, basic time series models, fundamentals of spatial random processes.
<font color="#95b3d7">环境数据的统计数据挖掘：数据分析方法包括主成分分析、线性回归、基本时间序列模型和空间随机过程基础。</font>

Stochastic models for carbon pricing and trading: Markov chain, binomial tree model, random walk, Brownian motion, Black-Scholes model, carbon asset pricing, derivatives pricing in carbon market.
<font color="#95b3d7">碳定价与交易的随机模型：马尔可夫链、二叉树模型、随机游走、布朗运动、Black-Scholes 模型、碳资产定价、碳市场中的衍生品定价。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

This subject mainly deliveries through lectures and programming training. The teaching and learning approach is mainly problem-solving oriented. The approach aims at the development of data analytics techniques and how the techniques can be applied to problem solving. Students are encouraged to adopt a deep study approach by employing high level cognitive strategies, such as critical and evaluative thinking, relating, integrating and applying theories to practice.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和编程训练进行。教学与学习以解决问题为导向，旨在培养数据分析技术，并说明如何将这些技术应用于问题求解。课程鼓励学生运用批判性思考、评价性思考、联系、整合以及将理论应用于实践等高阶认知策略，采取深度学习方式。</font>
