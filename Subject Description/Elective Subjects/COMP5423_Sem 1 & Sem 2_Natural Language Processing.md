# Objectives

The objectives of this subject are to enable students to acquire:
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在使学生获得：</font>

1. essential knowledge of natural language processing and its application scenarios;
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 自然语言处理及其应用场景的必要知识；</font>

2. practical techniques to handle and process natural language text;
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 处理自然语言文本的实用技术；</font>

3. fundamental skills to develop systems with natural language processing techniques and tools; and
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 使用自然语言处理技术和工具开发系统的基本技能；以及</font>

4. basic building blocks to design innovative software for different communities.
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 为不同社群设计创新软件所需的基本构件。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a. understand and apply fundamental knowledge and advanced techniques of natural language processing for textual information processing;
<font color="#95b3d7">a. 理解并应用自然语言处理的基础知识和高级技术来处理文本信息；</font>

b. develop creative solutions to solve technical problems through critical thinking, analytical thinking and creative thinking, leveraging emerging technologies for global challenges;
<font color="#95b3d7">b. 运用批判性思考、分析性思考和创造性思考提出创新解决方案，以解决技术问题，并利用新兴技术应对全球性挑战；</font>

c. demonstrate the ability to design, implement and evaluate effective natural language processing systems and applications to fulfill the real-world needs, demonstrating critical awareness and integrity in making responsible decisions; and
<font color="#95b3d7">c. 展示设计、实现和评估有效自然语言处理系统与应用的能力，以满足现实需求，并在作出负责任的决策时体现批判意识和诚信；以及</font>

d. engage in life-long independent learning for professional development, continuously adapting and reflecting on personal growth.
<font color="#95b3d7">d. 为专业发展开展终身自主学习，不断适应变化并反思个人成长。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Natural Language Processing Core Concepts
<font color="#95b3d7">自然语言处理核心概念</font>

Morphological Analysis, Syntactic Analysis and Parsing, Semantic Analysis, Discourse Analysis, and the Problem of Ambiguity.
<font color="#95b3d7">形态分析、句法分析与解析、语义分析、篇章分析以及歧义问题。</font>

Natural Language Processing Models and Techniques
<font color="#95b3d7">自然语言处理模型与技术</font>

Text Segmentation, Classification, Ranking, Sequence Labeling, N-Gram Language Model, Topic Model, Role of Machine Learning and Data Mining in Natural Language Processing, Natural Language Processing Tools, and Large Language Models (LLMs).
<font color="#95b3d7">文本分割、分类、排序、序列标注、N-Gram 语言模型、主题模型、机器学习与数据挖掘在自然语言处理中的作用、自然语言处理工具以及大语言模型（LLM）。</font>

Natural Language Processing Applications
<font color="#95b3d7">自然语言处理应用</font>

Machine Translation, Question Answering, Summarization, Opinion Mining and Sentiment Classification, Dialogue and Conversation, Reading Comprehension, Information Retrieval and Extraction, News Recommendation, etc.
<font color="#95b3d7">机器翻译、问答、摘要、意见挖掘与情感分类、对话与会话、阅读理解、信息检索与抽取、新闻推荐等。</font>

Selected Topics in Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning
<font color="#95b3d7">基于深度学习的自然语言处理选题</font>

Word Embedding (Word2Vec), Convolutional Neural Network, Recurrent Neural Network, Recursive Neural Network, Sequence to Sequence Model.
<font color="#95b3d7">词嵌入（Word2Vec）、卷积神经网络、循环神经网络、递归神经网络、序列到序列模型。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures teach students on the main concepts of the course, together with comprehensive examples, and class questions and answers for easy understanding.
<font color="#95b3d7">课堂讲授介绍课程的主要概念，并结合综合实例和课堂问答帮助学生理解。</font>

Tutorials and lab sessions help students to review the learned concepts, master the practical techniques and necessary tools for effective system/application development.
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课和实验课帮助学生复习已学概念，掌握有效开发系统或应用所需的实用技术与工具。</font>

Group project offers the opportunity to students to develop analytical and problem solving skills through system implementation and interpersonal communication.
<font color="#95b3d7">小组项目使学生有机会通过系统实现和人际沟通培养分析与问题解决能力。</font>
