# Objectives

(1) Introduce the concept of artificial intelligence and data analytics
<font color="#95b3d7">（1）介绍人工智能与数据分析的概念。</font>

(2) Equip the students with the fundamental knowledge of artificial intelligence techniques and data analytic algorithms in clinical applications
<font color="#95b3d7">（2）使学生掌握人工智能技术和数据分析算法在临床应用中的基础知识。</font>

(3) Master the clinical problem formulation and typical data analytic skills
<font color="#95b3d7">（3）掌握临床问题的表述方法和典型数据分析技能。</font>

(4) Understand the trend of technical development in medical artificial intelligence
<font color="#95b3d7">（4）理解医疗人工智能的技术发展趋势。</font>

(5) Be aware of the opportunities and risks in the field
<font color="#95b3d7">（5）认识该领域的机遇与风险。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes (Note 1)

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

1. Understand ideological and theoretical underpinning, the potentials and fundamentals of artificial intelligence and data analytic techniques in medicine;
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 理解人工智能与数据分析技术在医学中的思想和理论基础、潜力及基本原理；</font>

2. Describe the emerging and increasing healthcare demands for artificial intelligence and data analytics
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 描述医疗保健领域对人工智能与数据分析不断涌现和增长的需求。</font>

3. Articulate the major technological approaches of articular intelligence and machine learning to transform current healthcare service model
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 阐述利用人工智能和机器学习改造现有医疗服务模式的主要技术方法。</font>

4. Apply basic articular intelligence techniques and machine learning algorithm to tackle the given medical problem
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 应用基本人工智能技术和机器学习算法解决给定的医学问题。</font>

5. Understand the quality, regulatory, and ethical issues related to the use of artificial intelligence and data analytic technologies in medical domain.
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 理解在医学领域使用人工智能和数据分析技术所涉及的质量、监管与伦理问题。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus (Note 2)

▪ Landscape changes and opportunities- introduction of artificial intelligence and data analytic techniques for medical needs
<font color="#95b3d7">▪ 格局变化与机遇 - 介绍面向医疗需求的人工智能和数据分析技术。</font>

▪ The trend of artificial intelligence and data technology to transform current healthcare system
<font color="#95b3d7">▪ 人工智能与数据技术改造现有医疗保健系统的趋势。</font>

▪ Characterization of medical signals: feature engineering and extraction
<font color="#95b3d7">▪ 医学信号表征：特征工程与特征提取。</font>

▪ Basic understanding of supervised and unsupervised learning, neural networks, machine learning, and deep learning
<font color="#95b3d7">▪ 对监督学习、无监督学习、神经网络、机器学习和深度学习的基本理解。</font>

▪ Artificial intelligence and data analytic technologies to transform the healthcare service in the future of hospital.
<font color="#95b3d7">▪ 利用人工智能和数据分析技术改造未来医院的医疗服务。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology (Note 3)

<font color="#95b3d7">学生将在课堂讲授中学习基础知识和原理；实验课和导修课将提供充足的实践时间；并通过实践项目、作业和测验评估学生的学习成果。</font>
