# Objectives

The objectives of this subject are to:
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程的目标是：</font>

1. introduce the fundamental and advanced concepts and techniques of artificial intelligence (AI).
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 介绍人工智能（AI）的基础和高级概念与技术。</font>

2. solve important problems using comprehensive AI techniques
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 运用综合人工智能技术解决重要问题。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) build an extensive understanding of various advanced AI topics
<font color="#95b3d7">a）广泛理解各种高级人工智能专题。</font>

b) develop AI solutions, e.g., reasoning systems and deep learning methods, for real data-driven problems
<font color="#95b3d7">b）针对现实的数据驱动问题，开发推理系统和深度学习方法等人工智能解决方案。</font>

c) get an in-depth understanding of reinforcement learning, active learning and contrastive learning
<font color="#95b3d7">c）深入理解强化学习、主动学习和对比学习。</font>

d) apply advanced AI methods, e.g, transfer learning, ensemble learning, and meta-learning, to develop sophisticated deep learning models.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）应用迁移学习、集成学习和元学习等高级人工智能方法，开发复杂的深度学习模型。</font>

e) master the rationales and techniques for adversarial learning and federated learning
<font color="#95b3d7">e）掌握对抗学习与联邦学习的原理和技术。</font>

f) design explainable AI techniques for real-life problems
<font color="#95b3d7">f）针对现实问题设计可解释人工智能技术。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

1. Neural Networks and Deep Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 神经网络与深度学习：</font>

Neurons, Multi-Layer Perceptron, gradient descent, important concepts of deep learning, Recurrent Neural Networks, LSTM, GRU, Convolutional Neural Networks, Graph Neural Networks, Transformers
<font color="#95b3d7">神经元、多层感知机、梯度下降、深度学习的重要概念、循环神经网络、LSTM、GRU、卷积神经网络、图神经网络、Transformer。</font>

2. Reinforcement Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 强化学习：</font>

Important concepts of reinforcement learning, model-based learning, model-free learning, temporal difference learning, exploration vs. exploitation, regret, and policy search
<font color="#95b3d7">强化学习的重要概念、基于模型的学习、无模型学习、时序差分学习、探索与利用、遗憾和策略搜索。</font>

3. Knowledge representation and reasoning:
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 知识表示与推理：</font>

Rule-based production systems, case-based reasoning systems, model-based reasoning systems, reasoning under uncertain situations: stochastic methods and fuzzy expert systems
<font color="#95b3d7">基于规则的产生式系统、基于案例的推理系统、基于模型的推理系统、不确定情境下的推理：随机方法和模糊专家系统。</font>

4. Active Learning and Contrastive Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">4. 主动学习与对比学习：</font>

Definitions of active learning, active learning strategies and algorithms, contrastive learning objectives, algorithms for contrastive learning
<font color="#95b3d7">主动学习的定义、主动学习策略与算法、对比学习目标、对比学习算法。</font>

5. Adversarial Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">5. 对抗学习：</font>

Attack strategies and types, adversarial learning techniques, adversarial learning in deep neural networks, graph adversarial networks
<font color="#95b3d7">攻击策略与类型、对抗学习技术、深度神经网络中的对抗学习、图对抗网络。</font>

6. Transfer Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">6. 迁移学习：</font>

Concepts and applications of transfer learning, classic transfer learning approaches, transfer learning in various domains
<font color="#95b3d7">迁移学习的概念与应用、经典迁移学习方法、不同领域中的迁移学习。</font>

7. Ensemble Learning and Meta Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">7. 集成学习与元学习：</font>

Definitions of ensemble and meta learning, bagging and boosting in ensemble learning, multi-task learning, optimization-based meta-learning, Bayesian meta-learning
<font color="#95b3d7">集成学习与元学习的定义、集成学习中的装袋法与提升法、多任务学习、基于优化的元学习、贝叶斯元学习。</font>

8. Federated Learning:
<font color="#95b3d7">8. 联邦学习：</font>

Concepts of federated learning, distributed machine learning, privacy-preserving federated learning, and horizontal and vertical federated learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">联邦学习的概念、分布式机器学习、隐私保护联邦学习，以及横向与纵向联邦学习。</font>

9. Explainable AI:
<font color="#95b3d7">9. 可解释人工智能：</font>

Definitions and goals of Explainable AI, classic explainable methods (e.g., decision trees, Bayesian networks, sparse linear models), explainable deep learning techniques, and data visualization.
<font color="#95b3d7">可解释人工智能的定义与目标、经典可解释方法（例如决策树、贝叶斯网络、稀疏线性模型）、可解释深度学习技术和数据可视化。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

This course explores advanced AI techniques. It provides a comprehensive view of the problems and techniques of artificial intelligence. The course involves both theory and practice of AI topics. The course covers both classic AI algorithms and recent advances in the field. Lectures will contain sufficient analysis, examples, demos, and exercises. There will be tutorials or lab sessions to let students get hands-on experience on AI techniques. A fair portion of guided reading will also be provided.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程探讨高级人工智能技术，全面介绍人工智能的问题与技术，并兼顾相关专题的理论和实践。课程既涵盖经典人工智能算法，也涵盖该领域的近期进展。课堂讲授将包括充分的分析、实例、演示和练习；导修课或实验课将让学生获得人工智能技术的实践经验；同时还会安排适量的指导阅读。</font>

39 hours of class activities, including lectures, tutorials, labs, etc where applicable.
<font color="#95b3d7">共安排 39 小时的课堂活动，包括适用情况下的讲授、导修课、实验课等。</font>
