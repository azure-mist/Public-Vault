# Objectives

In scientific computing, we solve scientific and engineering problems posed in mathematical form using numerical algorithms/methods which can be implemented on computers.
<font color="#95b3d7">在科学计算中，我们使用可在计算机上实现的数值算法或方法，求解以数学形式表述的科学与工程问题。</font>

The aim of this subject is to provide students with basic training in computational methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在为学生提供计算方法方面的基础训练。</font>

The subject covers many important and widely used computational techniques and their practical applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程涵盖许多重要且广泛使用的计算技术及其实际应用。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Understand the theoretical and practical aspects of the basic algorithm for solving mathematical problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）理解用于求解数学问题的基本算法在理论和实践方面的内容。</font>

(b) Design programmes for numerical computation.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）设计数值计算程序。</font>

(c) Choose numerical methods so as to avoid or control error propagation.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）选择适当的数值方法，以避免或控制误差传播。</font>

(d) Apply the standard numerical methods for solving mathematical problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）应用标准数值方法求解数学问题。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Computer programming
<font color="#95b3d7">计算机编程</font>

A brief introduction to the language of MATLAB. Students are expected to use MATLAB to explore the vast area of application of numerical methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">简要介绍 MATLAB 语言。学生应使用 MATLAB 探索数值方法广泛的应用领域。</font>

Error analysis
<font color="#95b3d7">误差分析</font>

Absolute error, relative error, round-off error, truncation errors, error propagation and significant digits.
<font color="#95b3d7">绝对误差、相对误差、舍入误差、截断误差、误差传播和有效数字。</font>

Solutions of linear systems
<font color="#95b3d7">线性方程组的求解</font>

Gaussian elimination, LU factorization, Cholesky factorization, the conjugate gradient method, iterative methods, vector norms, matrix norms, convergence of iterative methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">高斯消元法、LU 分解、Cholesky 分解、共轭梯度法、迭代法、向量范数、矩阵范数以及迭代法的收敛性。</font>

Solutions of nonlinear equations
<font color="#95b3d7">非线性方程的求解</font>

Fixed point method, Newton’s method, the secant method, Broyden method, BFGS method.
<font color="#95b3d7">不动点法、牛顿法、割线法、Broyden 方法、BFGS 方法。</font>

Matrix eigenvalue problems
<font color="#95b3d7">矩阵特征值问题</font>

Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, inclusion of eigenvalues, power method, Householder tridiagonalization, the QR-factorization method.
<font color="#95b3d7">特征值、特征向量、特征值的包含区域、幂法、Householder 三对角化、QR 分解法。</font>

Interpolation polynomials
<font color="#95b3d7">插值多项式</font>

Polynomial interpolation, the Lagrange and Newton formula, error formula.
<font color="#95b3d7">多项式插值、拉格朗日公式与牛顿公式、误差公式。</font>

Numerical integration
<font color="#95b3d7">数值积分</font>

The rectangular rule, the trapezoidal rule, the Simpson rule and Newton-Cotes rules.
<font color="#95b3d7">矩形公式、梯形公式、辛普森公式和 Newton-Cotes 公式。</font>

Initial value problems for ordinary differential equations
<font color="#95b3d7">常微分方程初值问题</font>

Euler’s method, Runge-Kutta methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">欧拉法、Runge-Kutta 方法。</font>

Finite difference methods for differential equations
<font color="#95b3d7">微分方程的有限差分法</font>

Two-point boundary value problems, elliptic equations, parabolic equations, hyperbolic equations
<font color="#95b3d7">两点边值问题、椭圆型方程、抛物型方程、双曲型方程。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will be delivered mainly through lectures and tutorials. The teaching and learning approach is mainly problem-solving oriented. The approach aims at the development of mathematical techniques and how the techniques can be applied to solving problems. Students are encouraged to adopt a deep study approach by employing high level cognitive strategies, such as critical and evaluative thinking, relating, integrating and applying theories to practice.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和导修课进行。教学与学习以解决问题为导向，旨在培养数学方法，并说明如何将这些方法应用于问题求解。课程鼓励学生运用批判性思考、评价性思考、联系、整合以及将理论应用于实践等高阶认知策略，采取深度学习方式。</font>
