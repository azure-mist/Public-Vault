# Objectives

To provide a comprehensive introduction to quantum information and quantum computation. To present fundamental concepts and principles of quantum physics, quantum information and quantum computation. To present several well-known quantum algorithms. To provide a starting point for students who are interested in pursuing research in the emerging and fast-developing field of quantum computing.
<font color="#95b3d7">全面介绍量子信息与量子计算；讲授量子物理、量子信息和量子计算的基本概念与原理；介绍若干著名量子算法；为有意在量子计算这一新兴且快速发展的领域开展研究的学生提供起点。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) master the basics of quantum mechanics,
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）掌握量子力学基础；</font>

(b) understand the basic concepts of quantum information and the basic principles of quantum computing,
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）理解量子信息的基本概念和量子计算的基本原理；</font>

(c) familiarise with several standard quantum algorithms and apply them to develop more new quantum algorithms.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）熟悉若干标准量子算法，并运用这些算法开发更多新的量子算法。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Quantum Mechanics:
<font color="#95b3d7">量子力学：</font>

Dirac’s bra-ket notation, pure states, density matrices, Pauli matrices, quantum measurements, projective measurements, POVM measurements, composite quantum systems, quantum evolution, partial trace, quantum entanglement
<font color="#95b3d7">狄拉克左矢-右矢记号、纯态、密度矩阵、泡利矩阵、量子测量、投影测量、POVM 测量、复合量子系统、量子演化、偏迹、量子纠缠。</font>

Quantum Information:
<font color="#95b3d7">量子信息：</font>

Qubits, quantum noise, quantum operations, quantum channel, decoherence, master equations, Shannon entropy, von Neumann entropy, quantum teleportation, super-dense coding
<font color="#95b3d7">量子比特、量子噪声、量子操作、量子信道、退相干、主方程、香农熵、冯·诺依曼熵、量子隐形传态、超密编码。</font>

Quantum Algorithms:
<font color="#95b3d7">量子算法：</font>

Quantum gates, quantum circuits, controlled operations, universal quantum gates, quantum Fourier transform, phase estimation, amplitude amplifications, Grover’s search algorithm, Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm, Shor's quantum factoring algorithm.
<font color="#95b3d7">量子门、量子电路、受控操作、通用量子门、量子傅里叶变换、相位估计、振幅放大、Grover 搜索算法、Deutsch-Jozsa 算法、Shor 量子因数分解算法。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will mainly be delivered through lectures and tutorials. The theoretical background of quantum information and their applications to quantum computational algorithms are both emphasized.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和导修课进行，同时强调量子信息的理论背景及其在量子计算算法中的应用。</font>
