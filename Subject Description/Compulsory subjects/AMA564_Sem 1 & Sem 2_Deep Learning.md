# Objectives

The objective of this course is to equip students with the mathematical foundations and practical skills of deep learning, a core subfield of artificial intelligence. The curriculum will cover mathematical foundations of deep neural networks, key deep neural network architectures and training algorithms, computational tools, and real-world applications, emphasizing both the theoretical foundations and problem-solving skills in dynamic environments.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在使学生掌握深度学习这一人工智能核心子领域的数学基础与实践技能。课程将涵盖深度神经网络的数学基础、关键的深度神经网络架构与训练算法、计算工具及现实世界中的应用，并同时强调理论基础以及在动态环境中解决问题的能力。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes (Note 1)

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Understand the mathematical foundations of deep learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）理解深度学习的数学基础。</font>

b) Demonstrate mastery of the principles of machine learning and deep learning with mathematical theories.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）运用数学理论，展现对机器学习与深度学习原理的熟练掌握。</font>

c) Develop quantitative skills of deep learning and interpret the outcomes of deep learning algorithms.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）培养深度学习方面的定量分析能力，并解释深度学习算法的结果。</font>

d) Identify, define, and formulate problems of deep learning in real applications and generate workable solutions to problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）识别、定义并表述实际应用中的深度学习问题，并针对这些问题提出可行的解决方案。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus (Note 2)

Key topics to be delivered:
<font color="#95b3d7">主要讲授内容包括：</font>

a) The development history of deep learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）深度学习的发展历史。</font>

b) Mathematical foundations of deep neural networks: linear algebra, universality of neural networks, various loss functions.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）深度神经网络的数学基础：线性代数、神经网络的通用逼近性以及各种损失函数。</font>

c) Fully connected neural networks.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）全连接神经网络。</font>

d) Optimization algorithms: backpropagation, stochastic gradient descent and its variants.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）优化算法：反向传播、随机梯度下降及其变体。</font>

e) Convolutional neural networks: architecture, mathematical understandings, and applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）卷积神经网络：架构、数学原理及应用。</font>

f) Recurrent neural networks: architecture, autodifferentiation, and applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">f）循环神经网络：架构、自动微分及应用。</font>

g) Generative models: understanding VAE, GAN, Diffusion models from the statistical point of views and their applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">g）生成模型：从统计学视角理解 VAE、GAN、扩散模型及其应用。</font>

h) Preliminary Statistical Learning Theory: bias-variance trade-off; VC dimension; AUC scores.
<font color="#95b3d7">h）统计学习理论初步：偏差-方差权衡、VC 维和 AUC 分数。</font>

i) A brief introduction of large language model.
<font color="#95b3d7">i）大语言模型简介。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology (Note 3)

Lectures:
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：</font>

Introduce deep learning theory, algorithm derivations, and industry applications, with a focus on mathematical foundations and technical frameworks.
<font color="#95b3d7">介绍深度学习理论、算法推导和行业应用，重点讲解数学基础与技术框架。</font>

Tutorials:
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课：</font>

Hands-on coding sessions and group discussions on case studies, using real-world datasets and simulation environments (e.g., Pytorch).
<font color="#95b3d7">使用现实世界的数据集和模拟环境（例如 Pytorch），开展动手编程练习以及案例研究小组讨论。</font>
