# Objectives

To equip students with more advanced optimization algorithms for machine learning applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">使学生掌握更先进的优化算法，以应用于机器学习领域。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Gain deep understanding of optimization in the context of machine learning.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）深入理解机器学习情境下的优化问题。</font>

(b) Apply (deterministic and stochastic) first-order and second-order algorithms to unconstrained/constrained convex optimization, and large scale distributed optimization problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）将确定性与随机的一阶、二阶算法应用于无约束或有约束凸优化问题，以及大规模分布式优化问题。</font>

(c) Conduct computational complexity and convergence analysis and understand optimality and duality theory widely used for machine learning applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）开展计算复杂度和收敛性分析，并理解机器学习应用中广泛使用的最优性理论与对偶理论。</font>

(d) Formulate some machine learning problems in terms of optimization and solve them.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）从优化角度表述若干机器学习问题并加以求解。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Mathematical Background
<font color="#95b3d7">数学基础</font>

Applied linear algebra, theory of convex functions, convex and nonconvex optimization, sparse optimization. Examples include SVM, regression and convex clustering.
<font color="#95b3d7">应用线性代数、凸函数理论、凸与非凸优化、稀疏优化。示例包括支持向量机（SVM）、回归和凸聚类。</font>

First-order Methods
<font color="#95b3d7">一阶方法</font>

Gradient descent and accelerated variants, projected and proximal gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, Frank-Wolfe algorithm
<font color="#95b3d7">梯度下降及其加速变体、投影梯度下降与近端梯度下降、随机梯度下降、Frank-Wolfe 算法。</font>

Second-order Methods
<font color="#95b3d7">二阶方法</font>

Newton’s method, quasi-Newton methods, Semismooth Newton method
<font color="#95b3d7">牛顿法、拟牛顿法、半光滑牛顿法。</font>

Dimensionality Reduction Methods
<font color="#95b3d7">降维方法</font>

Principle component analysis and multidimensional scaling, ISOMAP, t-SNE.
<font color="#95b3d7">主成分分析与多维尺度分析、ISOMAP、t-SNE。</font>

Introduction of Duality Theory for Structural Optimization Problems
<font color="#95b3d7">结构优化问题的对偶理论导论</font>

Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions, Fenchel duality theory, Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM), distributed optimization algorithms.
<font color="#95b3d7">Karush-Kuhn-Tucker 条件、Fenchel 对偶理论、交替方向乘子法（ADMM）、分布式优化算法。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will be delivered mainly through lectures and tutorials. The teaching and learning approach are mainly problem-solving oriented. The approach aims at the development of mathematical techniques and how the techniques can be applied to solving problems. Students are encouraged to adopt a deep study approach by employing high level cognitive strategies, such as critical and evaluative thinking, relating, integrating and applying theories to practice.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和导修课进行。教学与学习以解决问题为导向，旨在培养数学方法，并说明如何将这些方法应用于问题求解。课程鼓励学生运用批判性思考、评价性思考、联系、整合以及将理论应用于实践等高阶认知策略，采取深度学习方式。</font>
