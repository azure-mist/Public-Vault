# Objectives

The objective of this course is to introduce students to emerging topics at the intersection of applied mathematics and artificial intelligence (AI). Specifically, the course aims to:
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在向学生介绍应用数学与人工智能（AI）交叉领域的新兴专题。具体目标如下：</font>

1. Expose students to recent advances and cutting-edge developments in artificial intelligence and applied mathematics, with a focus on emerging areas.
<font color="#95b3d7">1. 使学生了解人工智能与应用数学的最新进展和前沿发展，重点关注新兴领域。</font>

2. Examine contemporary techniques and methodologies in both artificial intelligence and applied mathematics.
<font color="#95b3d7">2. 探讨人工智能和应用数学领域的当代技术与方法。</font>

3. Equip students with practical skills and tools to develop AI models and perform data analysis across various disciplines.
<font color="#95b3d7">3. 使学生掌握开发人工智能模型以及在不同学科中开展数据分析所需的实践技能与工具。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Demonstrate a deep understanding of advanced concepts and challenges at the intersection of applied mathematics, artificial intelligence, and data science in emerging areas.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）深入理解新兴领域中应用数学、人工智能与数据科学交叉方向的高级概念和挑战。</font>

b) Apply mathematical modeling and analytical techniques, alongside AI methods, to address complex problems in cutting-edge applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）结合人工智能方法，运用数学建模和分析技术解决前沿应用中的复杂问题。</font>

c) Utilize mathematical and AI tools to analyze various forms of big data, and interpret the results in a visualizable way.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）运用数学与人工智能工具分析多种形式的大数据，并以可视化方式解释结果。</font>

d) Design and implement AI-driven solutions to novel real-world challenges, integrating mathematical reasoning and considering diverse requirements and constraints.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）综合数学推理并考虑不同需求和约束，为新型现实挑战设计和实现人工智能驱动的解决方案。</font>

e) Collaborate effectively in teams, and communicate technical findings through presentations and written reports, with an emphasis on the mathematical foundations of AI solutions.
<font color="#95b3d7">e）在团队中有效协作，并通过演示和书面报告交流技术成果，同时强调人工智能解决方案的数学基础。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

This course explores emerging topics at the intersection of applied mathematics and artificial intelligence (AI), with a focus on their applications across diverse scientific and industrial domains. Students will be introduced to advanced mathematical concepts and state-of-the-art AI techniques and will learn how these tools are transforming fields such as science, mathematics, finance, and healthcare. Through lectures, case studies, and hands-on projects, students will gain both theoretical understanding and practical experience in applying mathematical and AI methods to solve real-world problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程探讨应用数学与人工智能（AI）交叉领域的新兴专题，重点关注其在不同科学和工业领域中的应用。学生将学习高级数学概念与最先进的人工智能技术，并了解这些工具如何改变科学、数学、金融和医疗等领域。通过课堂讲授、案例研究和实践项目，学生将获得运用数学与人工智能方法解决现实问题所需的理论理解和实践经验。</font>
