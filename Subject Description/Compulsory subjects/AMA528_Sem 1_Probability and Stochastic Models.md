# Objectives

To enable students to have a thorough understanding of basic probability theory and some families of distributions, and their applications.
<font color="#95b3d7">使学生全面理解基础概率论、若干分布族及其应用。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

(a) Apply the concepts of probability, conditional probability and conditional expectations.
<font color="#95b3d7">（a）应用概率、条件概率和条件期望的概念。</font>

(b) Identify the distribution of random variable under various discrete and continuous distributions.
<font color="#95b3d7">（b）识别随机变量所服从的各种离散分布和连续分布。</font>

(c) Calculate probabilities, moments and other related quantities based on given distributions.
<font color="#95b3d7">（c）根据给定分布计算概率、矩及其他相关量。</font>

(d) Determine the probability distribution after transformation.
<font color="#95b3d7">（d）确定变量变换后的概率分布。</font>

(e) Apply stochastic models in mathematical modelling.
<font color="#95b3d7">（e）在数学建模中应用随机模型。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Fundamental probability: set function, sample space, events, set operation, probability, independence, conditional probability, three basic probability axioms: multiplication rules, law of total probability, and Bayes Theorem.
<font color="#95b3d7">概率基础：集合函数、样本空间、事件、集合运算、概率、独立性、条件概率，以及三项基本概率法则：乘法法则、全概率公式和贝叶斯定理。</font>

Random variables: discrete and continuous, distribution functions, expectation, variance, and higher order moments, moment generating functions, probability generating functions, cumulant generating functions and cumulants. Identify applications for which each distribution may be used, explain the reasons why, and apply the distribution to the application, given the parameters.
<font color="#95b3d7">随机变量：离散型与连续型随机变量、分布函数、期望、方差与高阶矩、矩母函数、概率母函数、累积量生成函数和累积量。识别各种分布适用的应用场景，说明原因，并在给定参数的情况下将相应分布应用于实际问题。</font>

Multiple random variables: Independence, jointly distributed, conditional distributions, marginal distributions. Conditional expectation, variance, and compound distributions; concept of Bayesian statistics; Apply techniques for creating new distributions: multiplication by a constant, raising to a power, exponentiation, mixing. Central limit theorem.
<font color="#95b3d7">多元随机变量：独立性、联合分布、条件分布和边缘分布；条件期望、方差和复合分布；贝叶斯统计的概念；通过乘以常数、乘方、指数变换和混合等方法构造新分布；中心极限定理。</font>

Stochastic processes: time index, ensemble average, autocorrelation, classification, stationary increment, independent increment. Markov property, Markov process, transition probability, multiple state Markov chain, application of Markov process models.
<font color="#95b3d7">随机过程：时间指标、系综平均、自相关、分类、平稳增量和独立增量；马尔可夫性质、马尔可夫过程、转移概率、多状态马尔可夫链以及马尔可夫过程模型的应用。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

The subject will be delivered mainly through lectures and tutorials. The teaching and learning approach is mainly problem-solving oriented. The approach aims at the development of probabilistic techniques and how the techniques can be applied to solving problems. Students are encouraged to adopt a deep study approach by employing high level cognitive strategies, such as critical and evaluative thinking, relating, integrating and applying theories to practice.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程主要通过课堂讲授和导修课进行。教学与学习以解决问题为导向，旨在培养概率方法，并说明如何将这些方法应用于问题求解。课程鼓励学生运用批判性思考、评价性思考、联系、整合以及将理论应用于实践等高阶认知策略，采取深度学习方式。</font>
