# Objectives

The objective of this course is to equip students with practical skills and algorithmic foundations in applying Artificial Intelligence (AI) to solve real-world problems in science and technology domains. The curriculum emphasizes hands-on implementation of AI techniques, covering machine learning, deep learning, and computational tools, with a focus on interdisciplinary applications in fields like science, engineering, healthcare, robotics, and others.
<font color="#95b3d7">本课程旨在使学生掌握运用人工智能（AI）解决科学与技术领域现实问题所需的实践技能和算法基础。课程强调人工智能技术的动手实现，涵盖机器学习、深度学习和计算工具，并重点关注其在科学、工程、医疗和机器人等领域的跨学科应用。</font>

# Intended Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to:
<font color="#95b3d7">完成本课程后，学生将能够：</font>

a) Identify key challenges and opportunities in applying AI to scientific research and technological innovation.
<font color="#95b3d7">a）识别将人工智能应用于科学研究和技术创新时的主要挑战与机遇。</font>

b) Understand the usage of AI algorithms and tools and their strengths and weaknesses in different scientific problems.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）理解人工智能算法与工具的使用方法，以及它们在不同科学问题中的优缺点。</font>

c) Demonstrate proficiency in implementing AI algorithms and tools for data analysis, modeling, and simulation.
<font color="#95b3d7">c）熟练实现用于数据分析、建模和仿真的人工智能算法与工具。</font>

d) Analyze and derive insights from real-world case studies in science and technology using AI methods.
<font color="#95b3d7">d）运用人工智能方法分析科学与技术领域的现实案例并从中提炼见解。</font>

# Subject Synopsis/Indicative Syllabus

Key topics to be delivered:
<font color="#95b3d7">主要讲授内容包括：</font>

a) Fundamentals of AI for science: machine learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow), scientific computing (NumPy, SciPy).
<font color="#95b3d7">a）面向科学的人工智能基础：机器学习框架（PyTorch、TensorFlow）和科学计算工具（NumPy、SciPy）。</font>

b) AI hands-on techniques for scientific data: regression, classification, clustering, image/signal processing, decision making and usage of LLMs.
<font color="#95b3d7">b）面向科学数据的人工智能实践技术：回归、分类、聚类、图像或信号处理、决策，以及大语言模型的使用。</font>

c) Case studies: Building large language model from scratch
<font color="#95b3d7">c）案例研究：从零开始构建大语言模型。</font>

d) Challenges (data quality, model interpretability, computational efficiency) and emerging trends (AI-driven scientific discovery, federated learning for science, and ethical AI in tech.)
<font color="#95b3d7">d）挑战（数据质量、模型可解释性、计算效率）与新兴趋势（人工智能驱动的科学发现、面向科学的联邦学习，以及科技领域的伦理人工智能）。</font>

# Teaching/Learning Methodology

Lectures:
<font color="#95b3d7">讲授：</font>

Introduce AI theories, tools, and interdisciplinary applications, with a focus on technical frameworks and scientific problem-solving.
<font color="#95b3d7">介绍人工智能理论、工具和跨学科应用，重点讲解技术框架和科学问题求解。</font>

Tutorials:
<font color="#95b3d7">导修课：</font>

Hands-on coding sessions and group projects using real scientific datasets, simulation environments, and AI development tools.
<font color="#95b3d7">使用真实科学数据集、仿真环境和人工智能开发工具开展动手编程练习与小组项目。</font>
